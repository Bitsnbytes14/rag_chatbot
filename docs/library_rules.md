# Library Rules

The library is open from 8 am to 8 pm, Monday to Friday, and from 9 am to 2 pm on Saturdays.
It is closed on Sundays and public holidays.

Each student can borrow up to 4 books at a time for 14 days.
Faculty members can borrow up to 10 books at a time for 30 days.
A book can be renewed twice, as long as no other student has reserved it.

The late fine is 5 rupees per day for each book.
A lost book must be replaced or paid for at its current price plus a 200 rupee handling charge.
Reference books and past exam papers cannot be taken out of the library.

Silence must be kept in the reading hall. Phones must be on silent mode.
Food and drinks are not allowed, except water in a closed bottle.
The group study rooms can be booked for up to 2 hours at the front desk.
Printing costs 2 rupees per page. Photocopying costs 1 rupee per page.
A valid student ID card is required to enter the library and to borrow books.
New book suggestions can be submitted to the librarian using the suggestion box.
