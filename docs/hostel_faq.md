# Hostel FAQ

The hostel gate closes at 10:30 pm on weekdays and 11:30 pm on weekends.
Students who return after closing time must sign the late entry register at the gate.
Visitors are allowed in the common room between 4 pm and 7 pm only.
Visitors are not allowed inside rooms at any time.

The monthly hostel fee is 6,500 rupees for a double room and 9,000 rupees for a single room.
The fee must be paid by the 5th of every month. A late fee of 200 rupees applies after that.
The mess deposit is 3,000 rupees and is refunded when the student leaves the hostel.

Breakfast is served from 7:30 am to 9 am, lunch from 12:30 pm to 2 pm, and dinner from 7:30 pm to 9 pm.
Laundry service runs on Tuesdays and Fridays. Clothes must be handed in before 9 am.

Cooking appliances, heaters and irons are not allowed in rooms.
Quiet hours are from 10 pm to 6 am. Loud music during quiet hours leads to a written warning.
Room changes can be requested at the warden office in the first week of each semester.
For maintenance problems, write the issue in the complaint register at the front desk.
The warden office is open from 9 am to 5 pm, Monday to Saturday.
